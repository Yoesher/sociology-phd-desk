## Why the project matters

Sociology researchers routinely combine reference management, writing, statistical analysis, qualitative analysis, spreadsheets, cloud storage, email, and journal submission systems. Those tools manage different files and operations, but rarely connect the research question, evidence, analysis decision, manuscript claim, submission, reviewer comment, and revision.

Sociology PhD Desk addresses that fragmentation with a local-first research orchestration layer. Its potential ecosystem value depends on whether it can make evidence provenance, qualitative traceability, quantitative reproducibility metadata, and peer-review revision work meaningfully easier for real researchers without centralizing sensitive research data.

That potential is a rationale for building and testing the project, not evidence of current adoption.
