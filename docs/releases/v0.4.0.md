## 简体中文

### 本地地图与研究草图

导入自己有权使用的静态 PNG/JPEG 地图或研究草图，在本地标注现有田野点，并查看关联的访问与访谈。支持点击、键盘与百分比输入，以及保存后刷新和离线继续研究。标注位置仅是图内示意；只使用县域或更粗的研究示意图，或匿名公共研究场所。禁止参与者住宅、精确位置与身份信息。

### 文献、项目与研究期限

- 本地 PDF、手动登记与 Zotero 元数据导入各有入口；文献支持查看和编辑，PDF 随记录、普通及加密备份保存。
- PDF 上限提高到单个 **10 MiB**、工作台合计 **20 MiB**，并显示容量用量；点击下载时才解码附件。
- 项目空间统一显示该项目的研究记录；写入与备份保留完整工作台和其他项目的数据。
- 任务可以重新查看和编辑截止日期、备注，并保留完成状态；到期/逾期天数与未来七天视图在打开应用时显示。页面跨本地午夜、重新聚焦或恢复可见时更新日期。

### 数据、隐私与容量

便携工作台、标准数据库和认证加密载荷为 **v7**；加密容器、vault 与 registry 数据库继续为 **v1**。旧工作台迁移保留已有研究数据、PDF 与 Zotero 来源；旧数据的地图集合为空，不推断任何位置。所有项目、附件与标注进入完整工作台备份。

地图图像最多 **2 MiB/个、4 MiB/工作台**，单边不超过 **8,192 像素**、总面积不超过 **1,600 万像素**。完整普通 JSON 上限为 **32 MiB**；不能只导出当前项目或截断附件以绕过上限。已有超限工作台仍可读取、迁移及在原有加密容量约束内保全；超限交互修改只允许不增加完整备份字节数。加密密文上限仍为 **64 MiB**。

应用不提供全国底图、行政区目录、在线地图服务、GPS 或地图图像公开导出；全国地图的来源、许可、审图与完整性门禁继续 BLOCKED。原始图像可能包含 EXIF/GPS 元数据，应用不移除这些内容；百分比标注也不保证匿名。无账号、默认云同步或分析追踪。更大规模的独立附件存储、分块认证备份与关闭网页后的系统提醒仍未实现。

### 使用与更新

浏览器访问是一等使用方式，可选 PWA 安装；本次不提供桌面安装包。已有用户打开应用后，可在确认安全写入完成后选择更新。请保留独立、经过验证的备份，不要将不可替代的材料仅存于一个浏览器配置文件。

Zotero 插件仍为 **0.1.0**，只导入书目元数据，不读取 PDF、笔记、批注、全文或账号。继续使用 v0.3.0 的[原始 XPI](https://github.com/Yoesher/sociology-phd-desk/releases/download/v0.3.0/sociology-phd-desk-zotero-0.1.0.xpi)和[SHA-256 文件](https://github.com/Yoesher/sociology-phd-desk/releases/download/v0.3.0/sociology-phd-desk-zotero-0.1.0.sha256)；本次不发布新插件资产，也不替换原资产。

## English

### Local maps and research sketches

Import static PNG/JPEG maps or sketches you are entitled to use, annotate existing same-project field sites locally, and view linked visits/interviews. Pointer, keyboard and percentage entry support saved/reloaded and offline research workflows. Positions are schematic image coordinates. Use coarse county-or-broader research sketches or anonymous public settings; participant homes, precise locations and identities are outside scope.

### Literature, projects and deadlines

- Separate local PDF, manual literature entry and Zotero metadata import. View/edit records and retain PDFs in standard/encrypted storage and complete backups.
- PDF bounds increase to **10 MiB per file / 20 MiB per workspace**, with capacity indicators and decoding only when downloading.
- Project spaces display relevant research records while every write and backup preserves the complete workspace and other projects.
- Reopen/edit task dates and notes without losing completion status. Due/overdue and upcoming-seven-day views refresh while the app is open, across local midnight and on focus/visibility recovery.

Portable workspace, standard database and authenticated encrypted payload advance to **v7**; encrypted container, vault and registry databases stay **v1**. Legacy migrations preserve records, PDFs and Zotero provenance and initialize empty maps without inferring locations.

Local images are bounded to **2 MiB each / 4 MiB per workspace**, **8,192 pixels per edge** and **16 million pixels**. Complete ordinary JSON remains bounded to **32 MiB**; no truncated or current-project-only backup is produced. Valid existing over-budget workspaces remain readable/migratable and preservable within the original encrypted limits; interactive over-budget writes must not increase complete-backup size. Encrypted ciphertext remains capped at **64 MiB**.

No national basemap/catalog, online map service, GPS or public map-image export is bundled. National source, license, map-review and completeness gates remain BLOCKED. Original bytes may retain EXIF/GPS metadata; percentage coordinates do not guarantee anonymity. No account, default cloud synchronization or analytics is introduced. Independent large-attachment storage, chunked authenticated backups and closed-page system notifications remain unimplemented.

The web app is canonical, with optional PWA installation and user-confirmed updates after safe writes; no desktop installer is provided. Keep independent verified backups. Zotero plugin **0.1.0** remains metadata-only: use the unchanged [v0.3.0 XPI](https://github.com/Yoesher/sociology-phd-desk/releases/download/v0.3.0/sociology-phd-desk-zotero-0.1.0.xpi) and [checksum](https://github.com/Yoesher/sociology-phd-desk/releases/download/v0.3.0/sociology-phd-desk-zotero-0.1.0.sha256). No plugin asset is republished.
