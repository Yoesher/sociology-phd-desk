# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Sociology PhD Desk contributors, maintainers, and participants are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, research tradition, methodological approach, or career stage.

We value substantive disagreement. Critique research assumptions, product decisions, and code; do not demean the people presenting them.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Make room for doctoral researchers, non-native English speakers, first-time contributors, and people outside software engineering.
- State uncertainty and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Respect research ethics, confidentiality, consent, and intellectual property.
- Keep private data and vulnerability details out of public issues and pull requests.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and focus on the community's long-term health.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or unwelcome attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing identifiable participant information, confidential field material, credentials, or embargoed research through project channels.
- Fabricating research evidence, test results, project metrics, contributors, adoption, or community activity.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone officially represents the project in public or private community spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If private GitHub reporting is available, use the repository's private reporting channel; do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in context, protect confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, and may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should recuse themselves. Bad-faith or knowingly false reports may themselves violate this code; an unproven good-faith report does not.
